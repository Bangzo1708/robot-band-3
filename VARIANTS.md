# Candidate variants — Name the Robot Band (round 3)

Judge criteria (most important first): funny robot band name + hit song words;
laugh on first read with a real joke; hit song line adds a second laugh and fits
the name; sounds like a band that could exist; few words, no filler, no
explaining the joke. All under 200 characters.

## Variant A
The Roombas — hit single 'Suck It Up', recorded live under your couch.

## Variant B
The Error Messages — hit single 'Something Went Wrong', which crashed at number one.

## Variant C
Ctrl+Alt+Defeat — hit single 'Have You Tried Turning It Off and On Again?'

## Assessment

- **Variant A — CHOSEN.** Strongest on every criterion. "The Roombas" is instantly
  visual, a real household robot, and a band name you would actually repeat to
  a friend. "Suck It Up" is a double joke (vacuum suction + the idiom) that
  lands on first read. "Recorded live under your couch" is a second laugh that
  fits the robot's real habitat and extends the joke without explaining it.
  Short, silly, no filler. Most original of the three: it avoids the tired
  "beep boop / 404 / generic robot" territory by picking a specific, mundane
  robot and letting the joke grow out of what it actually does.
- **Variant B — runner-up.** "The Error Messages" is a good band name and
  "crashed at number one" is a solid second laugh. But "Something Went Wrong"
  is a phrase everyone has seen a thousand times, so the first laugh is weaker
  than A's, and the joke leans on a generic computer-error trope rather than a
  specific robot.
- **Variant C — weakest.** The name is a decent pun and the song line is
  relatable tech-support humour, but it is a well-worn joke (turn it off and
  on again) with no robot-specific second beat, and "Ctrl+Alt+Defeat" reads
  more like a gamer tag than a band you would go see.

## Final choice
Variant A: "The Roombas — hit single 'Suck It Up', recorded live under your couch."
